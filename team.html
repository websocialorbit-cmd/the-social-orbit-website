01 Karan Varia

Karan Varia

Filmmaker. Editor. Storyteller.

IMDb in
Karan@thesocialorbit.in

Trained in Direction at the prestigious London Film School, Karan Varia brings over 15 years of experience across the spectrum of filmmaking—from theatrical trailers and promos to feature films, series, commercials, documentaries and music videos. His journey began with a deep understanding of cinema's craft and evolved into a career shaped by storytelling, precision, instinct and an uncompromising eye for detail.

Having worked on films including Kahaani, The Dirty Picture and Rascals, Karan has spent years navigating the creative and technical intricacies of the film industry, collaborating with talent, teams and storytellers across diverse formats and genres. He doesn't just understand how films are made—he understands why they work. With a filmmaker's perspective, an editor's instinct and a storyteller's obsession, he brings every project closer to the emotion, rhythm and vision it deserves.

02 Bharat Chandra

Bharat Chandra

Founder, Director & Colorist

IMDb v in
Bharat@thesocialorbit.in

Storyteller by passion, Colourist by craft, and Director by expertise. Bharat Chandra is a Mumbai-based filmmaker with over 7 years of industry experience dedicated to bringing vibrant colours and compelling narratives to the screen.

With a deep-rooted enthusiasm for the cinematic arts, Bharat has developed a reputation for a seamless workflow that bridges the gap between production and post-production.

Bharat's versatile career is highlighted by his substantial experience in direction departments. He served as an Associate Director on the project "Adhoora Poora," where he honed his skills in high-level creative execution and production management. He also contributed to the 2025 blockbuster commercial project "Out Karo" featuring Jasprit Bumrah and Mika Singh as the 2nd Assistant Director.

02 Bharat Chandra

Bharat Chandra

Founder, Director & Colorist

IMDb v in

Storyteller by passion, Colourist by craft, and Director by expertise. Bharat Chandra is a Mumbai-based filmmaker with over 7 years of industry experience dedicated to bringing vibrant colours and compelling narratives to the screen.

With a deep-rooted enthusiasm for the cinematic arts, Bharat has developed a reputation for a seamless workflow that bridges the gap between production and post-production.

Bharat's versatile career is highlighted by his substantial experience in direction departments. He served as an Associate Director on the project "Adhoora Poora," where he honed his skills in high-level creative execution and production management. He also contributed to the 2025 blockbuster commercial project "Out Karo" featuring Jasprit Bumrah and Mika Singh as the 2nd Assistant Director.
